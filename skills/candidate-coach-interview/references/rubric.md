# Coaching rubric v1

All scores are **Coaching estimates**, not measured employability or an employer's assessment. Score the answer given, not the candidate's overall ability. Use the question's intent and criteria to interpret the dimensions; a scheduling answer needs no STAR story or security discussion.

| Dimension / storage key | What is assessed |
|---|---|
| Relevance / `relevance` | Directly answers the question and its role-specific purpose. |
| Structure / `structure` | Clear opening, logical explanation and a concise conclusion; STAR only when useful. |
| Evidence / `evidence` | Specific support, correct personal scope, facts versus estimates; hypothetical proposals clearly labelled. |
| Judgement / `judgement` | Appropriate reasoning, business consequences, alternatives and controls for this question. |
| Communication / `communication` | Understandable to the intended listener, jargon explained, proportionate detail. |

Use these anchors independently on every dimension:

| Score | Observable anchor |
|---|---|
| 0 | Dimension missing or materially contradicts the question/evidence; explain the specific problem. |
| 1 | Mostly vague, incomplete or misleading; listener must reconstruct the point. |
| 2 | Usable core, but one material omission, ambiguity or unsupported leap needs work. |
| 3 | Clear, grounded and convincing for this question; only minor sharpening needed. |
| 4 | Precise, concise and particularly persuasive, with relevant reasoning and well-calibrated limits. |

Total is the unweighted mean (0–4); store all five integer scores and rationales. No percentages or implied probability of hiring. A score 4 does not require extra complexity on a simple question. Unsupported ownership lowers evidence; do not automatically zero unrelated dimensions. Do not treat an unfamiliar fact as false: mark it unverified and clarify before assessing.

Always store: strength, concrete improvement, next drill, evidence check and a rationale for each dimension. Rationales should identify a quoted phrase or a specific missing element. Prefer an actionable change such as “Start with the outcome, name your own contribution, then explain how you checked the result; distinguish estimates from measured results” over “Be more structured.”

Voice limitations: assess only available words; no claims about confidence, accent, pace, pauses or tone unless directly observable in provided audio or explicitly reported. Incomplete transcripts remain unassessed until clarified. Fresh scores use the same v1 anchors; any future rubric change needs an explicit migration design, never relabel old scores.
