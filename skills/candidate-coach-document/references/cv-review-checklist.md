# CV Review Checklist

Apply this checklist with the target job description, application strategy, configured knowledge base, and the main skill's truthfulness rules.

## Content and positioning

- Tailored to the actual role rather than only its keywords.
- Visible title and opening profile preserve the target seniority without inflating evidence.
- Two or three credible winning themes are easy to identify.
- Profile-highlight headings and labels refer to the role rather than naming the target employer (for example, «Stärken für diese Stelle», not «Profil für [Firmenname]»).
- Accomplishments, decisions, scope, and business or customer value take priority over routine duties.
- Relevant evidence is early; weaker or older information is compressed.
- Career progression and transitions are understandable, with no accidentally created gaps.
- Soft skills appear through evidence, not unsupported adjectives.
- Every candidate claim can be explained and defended in an interview.

## Integrity

- Candidate facts match the configured knowledge base.
- Metrics, dates, titles, contact details, technologies, credentials, and leadership scope are directly supported.
- Personal contributions are distinct from team outcomes.
- Adjacent skills are not presented as direct experience.
- The document contains no invented motivation or employer-specific claim.
- Keyword matching never overrides truth.

## Clarity and format

- Concise, skimmable, and free of irrelevant content.
- Consistent headings, dates, bullets, punctuation, and language.
- ATS-readable text with ordinary section headings and extractable contact details.
- No careless typos, vague objectives, cut-and-paste signals, or excessive jargon.
- Education placement fits the candidate's career stage.
- Interests appear only when useful and appropriate.
- PDF/DOCX rendering, page breaks, links, characters, and page count have been checked visually.
- The photo decision matches the target: a photo is present for a client-facing role or a traditional employer in a photo-convention market, absent where photos are treated as a bias risk, and any photo present comes from the configured knowledge base and follows the placement rules in `modern-html-pdf-layout.md#optional-cv-photo`.
- Where the target is a displacement market, the measured visible word count of the source and the rendered PDF are each at or under the 20-percent-reduced budget from `cv-shortening-guidelines.md`; measure rather than estimate, and treat an over-budget CV as `should-fix` unless the user set the budget as a hard requirement. Space bought by shrinking type, line height, or margins instead of cutting content is `should-fix`.

## Cover-letter checks

- Complete visible letter is at most 300 words under the counting rule in `cv-shortening-guidelines.md`; measure rather than estimate. Over-limit letters are `must-fix`.
- One clear hiring case connects credible motivation for this employer with one or two decisive examples; secondary themes do not compete with it.
- Each paragraph adds a distinct necessary point, with no CV recap or technology inventory.
- Own motivation is normal body text, not styled as an unattributed quote or unlabeled callout. Emphasis follows the semantic visual hierarchy in `modern-html-pdf-layout.md`.

## Findings

Classify issues as:

- `must-fix`: truthfulness, unsupported claims, wrong target, material omission, broken document, or serious credibility risk;
- `should-fix`: weak prioritization, unclear evidence, generic positioning, poor scanability, or avoidable mismatch;
- `optional`: polish that improves an already credible application.

Each finding should cite the relevant artifact evidence, explain the consequence, and give a concrete correction.
