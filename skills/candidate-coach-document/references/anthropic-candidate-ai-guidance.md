# Candidate AI Guidance

Primary source: [Anthropic — How to collaborate with Claude during our hiring process](https://www.anthropic.com/candidate-ai-guidance)

Source page last updated: 2025-07-10

Checked for this skill: 2026-09-02

## Application rules

- The candidate should create the first draft of application content. AI may help refine clarity, structure, wording, and selection of authentic experiences.
- Use AI to find which real experiences align with the role and to explain them more clearly.
- Do not use AI to create fictional experiences, accomplishments, credentials, metrics, motivations, or application answers detached from the candidate's own thinking.
- Preserve the candidate's judgment and voice; refinement must not turn into replacement.
- Be transparent in following the hiring organization's stated AI policy.

## Assessments and interviews

- Interview preparation with AI is encouraged: company research, practice questions, answer rehearsal, and topic review.
- Complete take-home assessments without AI unless the employer explicitly permits it.
- Use no AI during live interviews unless the employer explicitly permits it.
- If another employer publishes different rules, follow that employer's rules for its process while retaining the same truthfulness standard.

## Candidate Coach interpretation

Encouraged: map requirements to evidence, reorder sections, sharpen language, remove weak material, identify missing proof, and explain technical work plainly.

Not allowed: invent impact numbers, imply direct experience from adjacent technology, inflate leadership scope, create motivations, or hide material gaps.
